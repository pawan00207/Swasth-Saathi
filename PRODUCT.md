# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

Web, built with Next.js.

## Users

Patients and caregivers in India looking for healthcare services and ways to organize care. Individuals and organizations also use the legacy record workspace.

## Product Purpose

Help people explore government schemes, see hospital listings, plan accessible routes, find doctors and consultations, and manage appointments, reports and treatment bills.

## Operating Context

The landing page prioritizes finding services, as confirmed for this redesign. It keeps record identity setup available. The app labels seeded data and provider-dependent features. Medical reports and account workflows depend on sign-in and configured services. The SOS entry and existing emergency guidance remain available.
